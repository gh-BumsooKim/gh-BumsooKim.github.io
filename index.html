Biography (2026)

Bumsoo Kim received the BSc and MSc in art and technology from Chung-Ang University, South Korea, in 2023, 2026. He worked for machine learning algorithm and its industrial system at ETRI, VIVE STUDIOS, and Boeing Research & Technology, South Korea. His research areas include generative models on computer graphics.

Research fields: Multi-modal 2D/3D/Video generation and editing, audio-visual integration, differentiable rendering, and other artistic and gaming tasks.

Dynamic Projection Mapping | BS Graduation Exhibition (2023)


Computer vision technics + RGB-D camera
I developed a vision system that captures movable objects in real-time. To do that, I integrate RGB-D camera and video projector with GPU-accelerated Just-In-Time compiler on python interface. For visual contents, I optimized YOLACT model with arbitrary neural style transfer in edge devices.
